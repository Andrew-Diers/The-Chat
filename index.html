Chat help

Here are a few quick tips for using The Chat:

  • Type in the message box and press Enter or tap Send to post.
  • Double-tap a message on a touch screen, or double-click it, to open options for highlighting or bookmarking it.
  • Use Go to bookmark in the chat to jump back to your saved message.
  • Open Preferences to switch themes, choose an accent color, change message text size, and adjust sounds or notifications. Your accent color changes the app’s highlighted controls and accents.
  • Open Report a problem from the account menu to send a report to the admin.
